TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY

Submission. Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date through the finance portal. Claims submitted later than 30 days may be rejected. Receipts are required for every expense above Rs. 500.

Meals. During business travel, meals are reimbursed up to Rs. 1,500 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

Travel. Flights shorter than 6 hours must be booked in economy class. Business class may be used for flights of 6 hours or longer with director approval. Hotel stays are capped at Rs. 8,000 per night in metro cities.

Approvals. Any single expense above Rs. 10,000 requires prior written approval from the line manager. Expenses above Rs. 50,000 require department head approval.
