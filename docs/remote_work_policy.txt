REMOTE WORK POLICY

Eligibility. Employees may work remotely after successfully completing their 3-month probation period. During probation, employees are expected to work from the office.

Schedule. Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Teams must agree on common in-office days. All remote workers must be available during core hours of 11:00 to 16:00 local time.

Equipment and Stipend. The company provides a one-time home office stipend of Rs. 15,000 for a desk, chair or monitor. A monthly internet reimbursement of Rs. 1,000 is paid against a valid bill. Company laptops must use the VPN when accessing internal systems.
