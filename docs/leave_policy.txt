LEAVE POLICY

Annual Leave. Every full-time employee is entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next year; any remaining days expire on 31 March. Leave of more than 3 consecutive days must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance and approved by the line manager.

Sick Leave. Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required when sick leave extends beyond 3 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over.

Parental Leave. Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers are entitled to 6 weeks of fully paid leave. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption and may be split into two blocks with HR approval.
